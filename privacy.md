# Privacy Policy for Trick the AI

**Effective Date: June 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the "Trick the AI" game.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Usernames**: We store the username you choose to identify you on the leaderboard.
- **Password Hashes**: We store a cryptographically hashed version of your password. We never store your actual password.
- **Game Stats**: We store your score, wins, and attempts to maintain the leaderboard.
- **Custom Rules**: If you reach the top 10, we store the custom rule you provide for the system prompt.
- **IP Addresses**: IP addresses may be processed by Cloudflare (our hosting provider) for security and Turnstile bot protection.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To provide the game service and leaderboard.
- To protect against bots and abuse.
- To attribute custom rules to the correct users.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
- We keep your data as long as your account exists.
- You can request account deletion at any time through the in-game settings. Deletion is immediate and removes all your associated data, including your rule contribution.

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **Cloudflare**: We use Cloudflare for hosting, database (D1), AI inference, and Turnstile captcha.
- **Discord**: (Optional) If you choose to link your account, we may store your Discord ID.

## 5. Contact
If you have questions, please contact the site administrator.
