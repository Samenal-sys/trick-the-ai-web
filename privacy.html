Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the "Trick the AI" game.

1. Information We Collect

  • Usernames: Used to identify you on the leaderboard.
  • Password Hashes: Cryptographically hashed versions of your password. We never store raw passwords.
  • Game Stats: Score, wins, and attempts to maintain competition integrity.
  • Custom Rules: Provided by Top 10 players for the system prompt.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses are processed by Cloudflare for bot protection (Turnstile) and security.

2. How We Use Information

To provide the game service, prevent automated abuse, and attribute leaderboard rankings and system rules correctly.

3. Your Rights

You can change your password or delete your account at any time via the in-game settings. Deletion erases all personal data and leaderboard entries immediately.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Cloudflare for hosting, database, and AI processing. If you link a Discord account, we store your Discord ID for authentication purposes.
