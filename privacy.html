Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the "Trick the AI" game.

1. Information We Collect

  • Discord ID & Username: We use Discord OAuth for authentication. We store your unique Discord ID to link your account and your username to identify you on the leaderboard.
  • Game Stats: We store your score, wins, and attempts to maintain leaderboard rankings and competition integrity.
  • Custom Rules: If you reach the Top 10, we store the custom rule you provide for the AI system prompt.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses are processed by Cloudflare for security and bot protection (Turnstile).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the game service and maintain the leaderboard.
  • To prevent botting and automated abuse.
  • Social Integration: Game events (such as winning or updating a system rule) are logged to a public Discord channel. These logs include your username and the game messages involved.

3. Your Rights

You can delete your account at any time via the in-game settings. Deletion immediately erases all personal data, leaderboard entries, and custom rules from our database.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Discord: Used for authentication and public game event logging.
  • Cloudflare: Used for hosting, database (D1), and AI processing.
